🎣 HOW TO PLAY

← Hall of Fame · twitch.tv/langvaen

START IN 10 SECONDS

Come to twitch.tv/langvaen and type !cast in chat. You stand on the pier and catch a fish — its price goes to your coins. Your first cast ever is always a good one. Cast again every minute.

COMMANDS

!castfish (once a minute; the timer is by your name on the pier)
!neta fish jumped out of the water? the first to type it gets the fish
!duel @nick 300a fishing duel for 100–500 coins: 3 reels each, the dearer catch wins both stakes. !duel 300 = anyone may take it
!okaccept a duel (or !decline)
!meyour level, rod, coins and a link to your page on the Hall of Fame
!coinsyour balance
!shopthe shop on screen (once in 5 minutes)
!rod buythe next rod level: better luck for rare fish and more duel power
!head / !torso / !legs namedress your angler (each part bought once); !look shows yours
!shoothe seagull stole a catch? scare it off (10% of the fish for you)
GGright after someone catches a red or gold fish — 5 to 100 coins for the cheer
!helpa link to this page

FREE COINS AND LUCK

  • +100 coins for your first message each stream, +50 every 30 minutes in chat.
  • 🍀 Luck streak: every stream in a row you fish, +5% luck for rare fish (up to +50%). Rods add +20% per level.
  • 🎯 WANTED: one fish each stream pays double — see the sign under the pier.
  • 🐟 Chat goal: when the chat catches 100 fish together, a feeding frenzy starts for everyone.
  • Follow the channel: your next cast has double luck. A raid starts a feeding frenzy.

RARITY

white < light blue < blue < purple < pink < red < gold. Gold fish are one of a kind: whoever catches one owns it forever — it goes to LEGENDS on the Hall of Fame.

DUELS

Duel power = rod + level + luck streak; the screen shows the odds first. The one 10+ points behind gets a free re-spin of their worst reel, and if they win it's an upset with a bonus.
